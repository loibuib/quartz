A curated collection of technical runbooks, architecture patterns, and operational practices:

- **Security Operations & Observability:** Wazuh SIEM threat detection, Netdata metrics, Prometheus & Grafana monitoring stacks.
- **Data Protection & Compliance:** Vietnam Personal Data Protection Law (Decree 13/2023/ND-CP - PDPL), access governance, and endpoint encryption standards.
